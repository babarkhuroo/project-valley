Privacy

Project Valley · last updated 17 October 2026

Project Valley is a cosy village game. We keep only what the game needs to save your village and let you play with neighbours, and nothing else. There are no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

What we keep

  • Your village. Your game progress is saved on our server so it's safe if you change or lose your phone, with the time of each save.
  • A player id and sign-in token. Each phone gets a random player id and a token proving the village is yours. Tokens are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • An account, if you make one. Your username, the name you show in the game, and your password, which is stored only as a salted, one-way hash — nobody can read it, us included.
  • The Valley, if you join one. Your name, your village's name, what you deliver and the chat messages you send are seen by the other players in that Valley.
  • Network addresses. Our server briefly uses your IP address to stop abuse (for example, too many sign-in attempts). Our hosting provider keeps ordinary server logs.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, advertising ids or device identifiers. Reminders ("the Timber Yard is built") are worked out and scheduled on your phone; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Where it lives

The game's server and database are hosted by Render (render.com). Connections to them are encrypted (HTTPS).

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings → Help → Delete my village and account. Your account, sign-ins and village are deleted from our database straight away; in your Valley your name becomes anonymous and your chat messages are removed. What you delivered to Valley buildings stays, as those buildings belong to everyone there.

If you can't open the game, write to us (below) from the address you'd like the answer at, with your username or village name, and we'll delete it for you.

Children

The game is made for everyone. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children; please don't use your real name as a username.
